О проекте

umami — графики, дашборды и отчёты по данным бизнеса и сайта. Открытый исходный код.

umami: umami is a modern, privacy-focused analytics platform. An с открытым кодом alternative to Google Analytics, Mixpanel and Amplitude. In this catalog (Business), listed as “Analytics & BI”: Dashboards, BI, product analytics, web analytics. Key topics: Analytics, Audience Segmentation, Charts, Cohort Analysis, Google Analytics, and Product Analytics. Project details: freemium model, 38k+ GitHub stars, and built mainly in TypeScript.
